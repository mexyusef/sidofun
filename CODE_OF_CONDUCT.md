# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful, and
constructive.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or personal attacks
- discriminatory language
- deliberate disruption of collaboration
- bad-faith reporting or review behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull
requests, and contributions that do not meet this standard.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it through the
repository maintainers at:

- `https://github.com/mexyusef/sidofun`

Use private contact where possible for sensitive reports.
